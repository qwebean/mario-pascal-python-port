# Credits / 来源与许可

- **Original game**: *Mario & Luigi* by Mike Wiering (wieringsoftware.nl), Turbo Pascal 5.5,
  DOS VGA. The original game and source were released as freeware and may not be sold.
- **Main campaign**: the 23-level mod by Spenser Whaley (2005–2006); `levels.json` and the
  main-line behaviour follow this build.
- **Older builds / level packs**: `mario4` (2004 4-level build), `8g34`, `sunyu` and
  `webangelo` are converted from their respective executables / Flash sources; see
  [关卡来源.md](关卡来源.md) for details. `testrooms` contains levels made for this port.
- **Converted art**: some port-only sprites are derived from Mario XP, SMB3 reference art,
  91898 sheet art and the WEBANGELO mod. Only converted runtime data is shipped here;
  no original game executables, Pascal source, or third-party source artwork are included.

This port is provided for study and free sharing. No commercial use. Original game levels,
music and graphics remain the property of their respective authors.
